EMBER & EVER — BIRTHDAY WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- assets/photos/ (put your final portrait here)

HOW TO PERSONALISE:
1. Open script.js.
2. In CONFIG, change name and message.
3. Put the birthday person's main portrait at assets/photos/birthday-person.jpg (or change CONFIG.finalPhoto).
4. In CONFIG.photos, add up to 7 photo URLs and captions. For local photos, put them in assets/photos/ and use paths such as assets/photos/photo1.jpg.
5. Upload the full folder to GitHub. Keep all three files together and keep the assets folder.

THEME:
At Chapter 01 the visitor chooses MIDNIGHT AURORA (deep navy, teal and ice) or VELVET ORCHID (deep plum, orchid and pearl). The choice changes every chapter while keeping the same chapter order. Chapter 02 is a drag-to-connect constellation, Chapter 03 is a rotating light maze, and Chapter 05 is a hidden-crystal deduction game.

MUSIC:
Music starts after the first tap/click because mobile browsers block autoplay before user interaction.
